“Cantabile is the way we come to hear Truth in Bach.”

Truth is heard in relationships: bass, harmony, rhythm, voice-leading, counterpoint, phrase and form. Every voice must be capable of singing. The recordings are listening lenses, not authorities.

How to listen

First hear the work without interruption. On the next hearing, follow the score or text; sing a voice, then follow the bass, cadences, entrances and returns. Record one specific audible event, not simply whether the performance felt expressive.

Use the stage question as an inquiry, not an exam. At the piano, sing one voice while playing another. Beauty is not pursued directly; it emerges when the musical relationships are heard clearly.
